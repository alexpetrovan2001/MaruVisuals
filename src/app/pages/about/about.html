About Us

We are Marius and Ioana, a creative duo with a truly international vision. Having traveled to over 25 countries and collaborated with luxury hotels, resorts, and international brands across more than 10 countries, we bring a global perspective and cultural sensitivity to every project we create.

Marius Muresan

Marius Mureșan is a photographer, videographer, and creative director specializing in premium cinematic content for the hospitality and lifestyle industry. He collaborates with luxury hotels, resorts, and international brands to create natural, immersive visuals guided by light, emotion, and storytelling. With a background in law and consulting, Marius brings strategic thinking, precision, and structure to his creative work.

Ioana Manu

Ioana is a photographer and scriptwriter who creates compelling visual stories for luxury hotels, resorts, and lifestyle brands. Her background in pharmacy gives her a unique eye for detail and emotional depth, resulting in visuals that feel both precise and deeply human.
